    Before you ask

    Where exactly is CRE8?

    Inside Novva Industrial Park, Sungai Buaya, Serendah. Today you reach it from the Sungai Buaya Interchange on the North–South Expressway (E1) via Jalan Sungai Buaya.

    How big are the factories?

    Land from 0.99 to 1.25 acres (43,056 to 54,335 sq ft) and built-up from 22,833 to 25,865 sq ft, across three types. Type A plots measure 50 m × 80 m.

    What is the price?

    SPA prices run from RM 9,985,000 to RM 11,248,000, which works out to about RM 425 to RM 437 per sq ft of built-up area. Ask us for the lot-by-lot chart.

    When will the Bukit Beruntung connection be ready?

    It is a planned future connection through Novva Industrial Park with a target of Q1 2028. It is subject to infrastructure implementation and authority / developer progress.

    Who is the developer?

    CRE8 Development Sdn Bhd, a subsidiary of Legacy Maker Berhad.
